# Bathroom fittings buying guide

Choosing bathroom fittings is easier with a checklist. This short guide covers wash basins, taps and showers for Indian homes.

## Wash basins
Measure the available wall space and the position of the waste pipe before choosing between wall-hung, pedestal and table-top basins. Check that the tap hole matches your tap, and prefer a basin with an overflow hole.

## Taps
Look for a smooth ceramic-disc cartridge, a solid brass body and a durable chrome finish. In hard-water areas, choose fittings with easy-to-clean aerators.

## Showers
Check your water pressure first. Low overhead-tank pressure works best with air-injected or low-flow rain showers; high pressure suits a wider overhead rain shower.
